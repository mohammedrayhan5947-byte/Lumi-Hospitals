# Lumi Hospital website: Indian legal compliance

Status as of 1 October 2026. This is a working checklist for the web team and the hospital. It is not legal advice, so have the hospital's counsel sign it off before launch.
Legal text lives in `src/data/legal.js`. Anything unknown shows as **[to be provided by the hospital]**, driven by `BIZ.legal` in `src/data/site.js`.

## What changed recently
- **DPDP Rules 2025** were notified on **13 Nov 2025** (G.S.R. 843(E)). Rules 1, 2 and 17–21 (the Board) apply now. Rule 4 (Consent Managers) applies from **13 Nov 2026**. Rules 3 and 5–16 (notice, security, breach, retention, DPO contact, children, rights) apply from **13 May 2027**. The site adopts them now.
- DPDP Act **s.44(2)**, which deletes IT Act s.43A, is also in the 18-month tranche. **Until 13 May 2027, s.43A and the SPDI Rules 2011 still apply**, so the site meets both sets of rules.
- The **NMC RMP (Professional Conduct) Regulations 2023 were put in abeyance on 23 Aug 2023**. The **IMC (Professional Conduct, Etiquette and Ethics) Regulations 2002** are the code in force. We follow the stricter reading of both.
- *Rajive Raturi v. UoI* (SC, 8 Nov 2024) held that the accessibility standards under RPwD Rule 15 must be mandatory, not advisory. Treat GIGW 3.0 / WCAG 2.1 AA as binding.
